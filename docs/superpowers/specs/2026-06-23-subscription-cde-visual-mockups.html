③ Drawdown chart · QD5

Credit drawdown — delivered solid, planned dashed, dual axis

needs B Bars = pcs/day (left axis), line = credit remaining in Rp (right axis). Solid = delivered (Mon–Wed), dashed + lighter = planned (Thu–Sun), with a "today" divider. Flags leftover credit if the line won't reach zero by Sunday. ✓ RESOLVED — per-subscription, NOT summed a selector picks ONE subscription; the chart titles it + links to its page (supersedes the design's "sum pools for display").

Credit line lands above zero by Sunday → under-ordered → leftover-credit flag fires. (Computed from pool.creditRemaining projected, not week.shortfall which only exists post-reconcile.)

✓ Resolved (c4): one subscription at a time via a selector/dropdown — title names it, small link to its subscription page (breadcrumb-consistent). No summed roll-up; each pool stays ring-fenced. This removes the multi-subscription alignment problem entirely.

⑦ Kanban read-only · Q12 (Phase E)

How does a subscription order look on the production kanban?

Subscription orders flow through the normal kitchen kanban but render in a distinct 🔒 Subscription style: no edit/status/delete controls, no pricing/credit shown (staff reach this board), and a single "Open in scheduler" link. The decision: do kitchen production controls (mark produced, auto-advance) stay enabled, or is the card fully read-only?
