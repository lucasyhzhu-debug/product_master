- But `/journal` route requires `canManageReimbursements` (admin-only)
- **Impact:** UX dead-end, not a data risk. Backend further guards with `protectedMutation`.

- No Header dropdown entry exists (unlike Business Settings, Asset Register)
- **Impact:** Discoverability gap. No requirement mandates Header presence.
