## Decisions Made

- Fallback branch (order deleted) leaves `totalDeliveryFees` at 0, matching the conservative approach for missing data
- Card uses `text-muted-foreground` on the value (not red like Commissions/Discounts) because delivery fees are neutral pass-throughs, not losses
- Loading skeleton updated alongside grid width change for consistent UX during load
