**Section 8: FAQ (`id="faq"`):**
- Full FaqAccordion with 5 groups, 16 total questions. Use ReactNode (JSX) for answers that need bold text or account codes. Use plain strings for simple answers.

Groups:
1. General (4 items):
   - "Do I need a Frollie Pro account to have a payroll account, or are they separate?" -- There is no "Frollie Pro." All features available based on role. Payroll is admin-only.
   - "Who can see my expenses?" -- You see your own. Managers/admins see submitted expenses in approval queue. Admins can see all.
   - "What does 'voided' mean?" -- Entry cancelled, accounting impact reversed. Original stays for audit but has no financial effect.
   - "Can I delete an expense?" -- No. You can void it (admin only). Deletion not supported for audit trail integrity.

2. Submission (3 items):
   - "What payment method should I choose?" -- Personal Cash/Transfer if you paid from your own money (reimbursed). Company Card if used company bank card (no reimbursement).
   - "I forgot to submit an expense from 3 weeks ago, is it too late?" -- No, but gets "Late Submission" flag. Not blocked.
   - "Can I submit expenses in foreign currency?" -- Not yet. All amounts in IDR. Convert using exchange rate on expense date.

3. Approval (3 items):
   - "Why can't I see some expenses in my approval queue?" -- Managers only approve <=500K. Higher needs admin. Can't approve own.
   - "What do the fraud warning badges mean?" -- See Expense Analytics section above.
   - "I rejected an expense by mistake. What do I do?" -- Submitter can revise and resubmit. You'll see rejection history.

4. Reimbursement (3 items):
   - "What if the bank transfer fails?" -- Void the batch. Creates reversing entry, returns expenses to pending. Re-batch and try again.
   - "Can I combine expenses from different employees in one batch?" -- No. One batch per employee. System enforces this.
   - "How does the submitter know they've been reimbursed?" -- Expense status changes to "Reimbursed" (real-time update).

5. Payroll (3 items):
   - "Do staff members need Frollie accounts?" -- No. Payroll entries are financial records. Recipient name is free text.
   - "How do I handle pro-rata pay?" -- Calculate yourself. Enter final amount.
   - "What's the difference between Staff and Contractor?" -- Classification for reporting. Both create same JE (DR 6100, CR 1100).
