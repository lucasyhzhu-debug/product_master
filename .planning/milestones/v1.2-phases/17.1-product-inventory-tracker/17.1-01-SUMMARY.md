## Decisions Made
- productInventory is a simple aggregate table (not FIFO batch) — quantity can go negative for GoFood outlets (sales can outpace replenishment)
- Reuse existing "Legato Goldfinch" venue location for GoFood Goldfinch mapping — no duplicate depot created
- Manager adjustStock allows negative stock (override mode) — reason field annotated with "[Manager override: stock went negative]" note
- initializeSettings is `internalMutation` — only called from seed, not exposed to frontend directly
