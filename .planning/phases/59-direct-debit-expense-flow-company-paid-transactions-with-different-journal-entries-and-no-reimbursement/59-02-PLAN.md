Update the expense frontend to reflect the simplified 2-option payment model, show conditional transaction reference field for company_paid, render the new `recorded` status badge, and replace Approve/Reject with Acknowledge/Flag actions for company_paid recorded expenses in the approval queue.

Purpose: Admins need to see company-paid expenses in their queue and acknowledge them (confirming the record is correct) or flag them for review, distinct from the approve/reject flow used for employee-paid expenses.

Output: Updated expense submission form, approval queue, and status components.
