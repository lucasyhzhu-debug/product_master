<objective>
Separate delivery fees from product revenue in Sales Analytics so that delivery fees appear as their own summary card and do not inflate Net Sales figures.

Purpose: Delivery fees are pass-through costs (collected from customers, paid to drivers). Currently they are baked into `finalTotal` and inflate the Net Sales figure. This fix computes the separation at query time using existing schema fields.

Output: Backend returns `totalDeliveryFees` alongside existing metrics; frontend shows a 5-card summary row with a new "Delivery Fees" card.
</objective>

   // CURRENT (buggy — delivery fee inflates net):

   ```typescript

   totalDiscounts += order.totalAmount - netProduct;

   ```
