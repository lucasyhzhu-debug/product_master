**Moderate removal candidates (remove if confirmed unused AND not needed for planned features):**
6. `orderItemProduction.by_remaining` -- grep for `withIndex("by_remaining"`
7. `orderItemProduction.by_completion` -- grep for `withIndex("by_completion"`
8. `productionProductTargets.by_date_product` -- grep for `withIndex("by_date_product"` (check if it's just a prefix of by_date_source_product)
9. `productionTargetLogs.by_date` -- grep for `withIndex("by_date"` in context of productionTargetLogs (careful: other tables use "by_date")
10. `productionTargetLogs.by_date_timestamp` -- grep for `withIndex("by_date_timestamp"`
11. `inventoryBatches.by_status` -- grep for `withIndex("by_status"` in context of inventoryBatches
12. `inventoryBatches.by_location` -- grep for `withIndex("by_location"` in context of inventoryBatches (check if by_component_location covers it)
13. `componentTransactions.by_batch` -- grep for `withIndex("by_batch"`
