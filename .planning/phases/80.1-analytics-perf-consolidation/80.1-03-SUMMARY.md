## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] skuPareto test assertion was testing `cap=20 + custom topN=10 + Other row` contract that no longer exists**
- **Found during:** Task 23 step 23.4 (npm run test)
- **Issue:** Old test seeded 12 products and asserted `rows.length === 11` (10 top + "Other"). Under the snapshot contract, `skuSnapshot.skuTop` is produced by `reduceSkuTop(current, pre, SKU_SNAPSHOT_TOP_CAP=20)`. With 12 products and cap=20, ALL 12 fit in the top list — there is no "Other" row. Asserting `rows[10].name === "Other"` would fail.
- **Fix:** Assertion tightened to `rows.length >= 12` and the `Other` row check dropped. Monotonic cumulativePct and final value close-to-100 assertions preserved — those are the invariants the test was actually guarding.
- **Files modified:** `tests/convex/unitEconomics.test.ts`
- **Commit:** 4492419a

**2. [Scope] Wave A left `collapseOvernight` in DayHourHeatmap.tsx (not in the reducer)**
- **Acknowledged:** The plan's critical_finding #4 noted the transform status was ambiguous. Wave A SUMMARY deviation #3 documented the decision to keep it component-side. Task 20 honored that decision — the new Nivo component has its own `collapseOvernight` (ported verbatim from the old component) applied inside the `useMemo` before passing data to `ResponsiveHeatMap`. Reducer signature is unchanged; emits raw 7×8 grid.
