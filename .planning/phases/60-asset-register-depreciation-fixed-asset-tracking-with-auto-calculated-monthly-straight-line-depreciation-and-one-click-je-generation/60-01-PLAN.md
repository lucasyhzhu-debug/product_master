    4. **calculateFinalMonthAmount(monthlyAmount, accumulatedDepreciation, depreciableAmount)** — returns min(monthlyAmount, depreciableAmount - accumulatedDepreciation). Handles the final-month remainder (Pitfall 2).

    5. **computeMissingMonths(acquisitionDate, lastDepreciationMonth, currentYYYYMM, usefulLifeMonths, accumulatedDepreciation, depreciableAmount)** — returns array of "YYYY-MM" strings for months that need depreciation.
