## Tests

### 1. Finished Goods tab appears in InventoryManager
expected: Open the Inventory page. The top-level tabs show three options: Packaging, Ingredients, and Finished Goods. Clicking "Finished Goods" switches to the new tab without any errors.
result: pass

### 2. Empty state on first visit
expected: With no stock added yet, the Finished Goods tab shows an empty state (prompt to add first stock). No errors or blank white screen.
result: pass

### 3. Add Stock dialog — staff flow
expected: Click "Add Stock". A dialog opens with a product selector, quantity input, and location selector. Select a menu product, enter a quantity (e.g. 5), pick a location, and submit. A success toast confirms "Added 5 x {product} to {location}". The stock card for that product appears in the grid.
result: issue
reported: "Product dropdown shows all menu products (Original, Bite Sized Single, Double, Triple, Jumbo, etc.) — should only show the 2 active POS items: Original Single (45g) and Original Triple (135g). Also shows Brochure - How to eat which is not a food product."
severity: major
notes: "Dialog functionally works (add stock + toast + card appears) — only the product list filtering is wrong"

### 4. Product stock card layout
expected: Each product with stock shows a card with: product name, total quantity badge (green/amber/red based on level), and a per-location row showing how many units are at each location. Low-stock cards have amber borders; negative-stock cards have red borders.
result: issue
reported: "Location selector in Add Stock / Adjust Stock dialogs shows a dropdown — should be 3 toggle buttons (Office, Kitchen, Legato Goldfinch) since there are only 3 locations."
severity: minor

### 5. Low-stock alert banner
expected: When a product's quantity is at or below the low-stock threshold (default: 10), a red alert banner appears at the top of the Finished Goods tab listing the affected products by name.
result: issue
reported: "No low-stock warning or banner appears even with stock at 2 (below any reasonable threshold). Stock card badge shows green '2' instead of amber/red. Default threshold is 5 not 10. Also: Adjust dialog shows no current stock quantity at the selected location — user can't see how much is there before adjusting."
severity: major

### 6. Adjust Stock dialog — manager flow
expected: On a product card (logged in as manager or admin), click "Adjust". A dialog opens with product/location pre-filled, Add/Remove toggle buttons, a quantity field, and a required reason textarea. Enter a reason and submit. Stock updates immediately and the transaction appears in history.
result: pass

### 7. Transaction history per product
expected: On a product card, click "History". A transaction log expands showing entries for: additions (emerald), drawdowns (blue), gofood_sale (purple), and adjustments (amber). Each row shows type, signed quantity (+/-), location, reason, and timestamp.
result: pass

### 8. Settings panel (manager/admin only)
expected: As manager or admin, a "Settings" toggle appears in the Finished Goods action bar. Clicking it reveals an inline settings card with: global low-stock threshold field and default location selector. Changes save successfully.
result: issue
reported: "Settings save fine but user is unsure what 'Auto-advance on Drawdown' and 'Alert Mode' do — labels/descriptions not clear enough."
severity: cosmetic

### 9. "Use Available Inventory" button on OrderDetail — PaymentReceived only
expected: Open an order with status "Payment Received" (Confirmed). In the sidebar, a blue "Use Available Inventory" card/button is visible. Open an order in any other status (e.g. In Production, Boxed) — the button is NOT visible.
result: issue
reported: "'Use Available Inventory' button does not appear on a PaymentReceived order. 'Force Complete (admin)' button also missing. Actions section only shows Expedite Production and Cancel Order. Also: after drawdown, should show confirmation 'xx used, yy left' per product type."
severity: major

### 10. Inventory availability panel before drawdown
expected: Click "Use Available Inventory" on a PaymentReceived order. A panel shows a location selector and a table listing each order item with columns: Product, Needed, Available, Status. Items with sufficient stock show a green "OK"; items short show a red "Short N" indicator.
result: skipped
reason: Depends on Test 9 button which is missing — will retest after fix

### 11. Successful drawdown — order advances to AwaitingDelivery
expected: With sufficient stock for all items, select a location and confirm the drawdown. A success toast appears. The order status updates to "Awaiting Delivery". The "Use Available Inventory" button disappears (no longer PaymentReceived). Stock quantities decrease in the Finished Goods tab.
result: skipped
reason: Depends on Test 9 button which is missing — will retest after fix

### 12. Insufficient stock error display
expected: Attempt drawdown on an order where at least one item has less stock than needed. The confirm button is disabled (grayed out) when any item is short. If you somehow trigger it, a red error alert lists the specific shortage: "{Product}: need 3, have 1".
result: skipped
reason: Depends on Test 9 button which is missing — will retest after fix
